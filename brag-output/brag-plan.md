# Brag Plan: CasaVault

## What is this app?
CasaVault is a statute-aware rental record for Philadelphia — upload your lease, it checks every clause against 9 verified PA/Philly statutes and cites the exact section behind each finding, tracks statutory deadlines, and has a grounded AI agent that answers questions from your records only or refuses and hands you to the Philly Tenant Hotline.

## The angle
Not a legal chatbot. Not a template generator. This is the anti-AI product: it cites or it shuts up. The most impressive thing CasaVault does is say "I can't answer that — here's who can." In a world of hallucinating legal AI, the refusal IS the feature. Show the product being right, then show it being honest.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
The statistic that sets the stakes — "87% of landlords have a lawyer. 16% of tenants do." — slam-cut onto the warm paper background. Then: "They don't lose because they're wrong."

## Key moments (the middle)
- A lease uploaded → three red violation cards slam in one by one, each with its PA statute citation in monospace: `68 P.S. § 250.511a(a)`, `68 P.S. § 250.511a(f)`, `68 P.S. § 250.511b`. The citations are the star.
- Agent Q&A: "How long does he have to return my deposit?" → "30 days" with citation `68 P.S. § 250.512`. Shows the grounded answer.
- The refusal: "Will I win in court?" → "I can't answer that. Here's who can: Philly Tenant Hotline (267) 443-2500." The punchline of the product.

## Outro / punchline
"Every answer cites a source. Every refusal routes to a human." — then the CasaVault wordmark, understated, on the warm paper ground. "Built at LexHack 2026."

## User flow worth showing
Upload lease PDF → red flags appear with statute citations → ask a question → get a grounded answer with citation → ask an unanswerable question → refusal + hotline handoff.

## Tone
- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: quiet civic tech that takes rights seriously — documentary gravity, not startup energy
- Interpretation: Restrained pacing, longer holds, serif presence. Confidence through stillness. The product's severity earns the viewer's attention, not speed or flash.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 22 seconds

Upload your lease. Know your rights. Every answer cites the law — or refuses and tells you who can help. Built at LexHack 2026.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm bed, restrained accents
- Music: `happy-beats-business-moves-vol-11-by-ende-dot-app.mp3` — warm, steady, not overpowering. Supports the gravity without fighting it.
- Music treatment: fade in from 0s, low-mid volume, steady bed throughout, gentle fade at outro. Not energetic — present.
- Music cue guidance: vol-11 preset, 114.84 BPM. Strong cues at 1.60s (hook text land), 5.80s (scene 2 transition — lease upload), 8.96s (first violation card), 12.65s (agent answer reveal), 17.91s (refusal moment). Beat grid for violation cards: ~8.96s, ~9.50s, ~10.01s (three cards, one per beat).
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; a faint glow/presence breathe on the finding cards timed to bass. Nothing showy.
- SFX posture: sparse, professional. Motion-matched card arrivals, one dry interface tick per violation card. A soft whoosh on the refusal transition.
- Audio-coupled moments: three violation cards land one per beat; the refusal text could have a single understated thud.
- Restraint rule: never louder than the content. No swooshes on text. No rise-and-drop on the hook. The product is about trust — the audio must not undermine that.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — The Stakes — 3.5s
Warm paper background (#f6f4ef). Large serif text, center-aligned:
Line 1: **"87% of landlords have a lawyer."** — appears first, holds.
Line 2: **"16% of tenants do."** — appears below, smaller.
Beat. Then a third line fades in, subdued:
**"They don't lose because they're wrong."**
Sequential/interaction: yes — three lines appear in sequence, each holding
Audio intent: weight, gravity, the problem is real
Audio-coupled idea: each line lands near a beat; the third line is quieter, no accent
Music: warm bed fading in from silence
Transition mood: soft → Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Upload + Flags — 6s
Recreated UI: a card on the warm paper ground. A lease filename "lease_north_broad.pdf" appears at the top. Below it, three finding cards slam in one by one, left-bordered red (#9f2f24) on violation-tinted background (#fdf0ee):
- Card 1: **"Deposit exceeds first-year cap"** — `68 P.S. § 250.511a(a)` in monospace
- Card 2: **"Deposit waiver clause is void"** — `68 P.S. § 250.511a(f)` in monospace
- Card 3: **"No escrow bank disclosed"** — `68 P.S. § 250.511b` in monospace
Each card has the red left border and the citation below in monospace. The citations are the visual star — they prove this is real, not vibes.
Sequential/interaction: yes — three violation cards arrive one by one, ~0.5s apart, each holding after entry
Audio intent: impact, each card is a finding landing
Audio-coupled idea: three cards on consecutive beats; a subtle interface tick on each
Music: bed steady, cards land on beat grid (~8.96, ~9.50, ~10.01)
Transition mood: clean → Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Grounded Agent — 5.5s
Recreated UI: the "Ask this vault" panel. A question types in:
**"How long does he have to return my deposit?"**
Answer appears below:
**"30 days from move-out."** with `68 P.S. § 250.512` in monospace beneath.
Hold to read. Then the question changes:
**"Will I win in court?"**
Answer: **"I can't answer that."**
Below, smaller: **"Here's who can: Philly Tenant Hotline (267) 443-2500"**
Sequential/interaction: yes — question types, answer reveals, then second question types, refusal appears
Audio intent: the pivot — from competence to honesty. The refusal is the payoff.
Audio-coupled idea: typing simulation on the question; the refusal could land with one dry thud
Music: bed continues, slight swell as the refusal lands (~17.91s strong cue)
Transition mood: soft → Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Closer — 4s
Clean warm paper. Two lines of serif text, centered:
**"Every answer cites a source."**
**"Every refusal routes to a human."**
Hold. Then the CasaVault wordmark fades in below, small and restrained. Beneath it, small sans: **"Built at LexHack 2026"**
Sequential/interaction: yes — two statement lines, then wordmark
Audio intent: resolution, quiet confidence
Audio-coupled idea: none — let it breathe
Music: bed fades to silence through the last 2s
Transition mood: end

**Music mood for this video:** warm, steady, restrained
**Audio summary:** A low warm bed throughout, never competing with text. Three dry ticks on the violation cards, one subtle thud on the refusal, silence at the end. The audio says "this is serious" without saying "this is dramatic."
